Import your offline transactions

Export a CSV from your bank yourself, then adapt it to this template. No credentials, online sync, or Apple Pay feed.

date,type,account,category,amount,description

Example: 2026-11-01,expense,Chase checking,Food,24.50,Groceries

Exact existing account/category names; type is expense or income. Blank expense category uses your first matching rule, then Other. Up to 2000 rows / 1 MB. Exact duplicate entries are skipped (same date, type, account, category, amount, description); legitimate identical purchases should be entered manually.

Automatic categories, locally

When an expense has no explicit category, its description is matched against these keywords, first rule first. Existing entries are not changed.

Backups & Excel export

Files are saved only in BudgetMe\app\exports, never your browser Downloads folder.

Backup files require the same passphrase. Backups on this PC do not protect against disk failure. Use the documented offline restore command.

Excel exports are NOT encrypted. Anyone who can read that file can read your finances. Avoid cloud-synced folders. Delete plaintext exports when no longer needed.

Opens in Excel as a multi-sheet workbook. It is SpreadsheetML (.xml), not .xlsx. All cells are literal text to prevent imported formulas from executing.

Privacy is a boundary, not a promise.

Your vault uses AES-256-GCM encryption and scrypt key derivation. It stays on this PC and is readable only after unlocking with your passphrase.

The app binds only to 127.0.0.1. No analytics, remote fonts, CDNs, telemetry, or financial-provider connections are implemented.

Windows malware, administrator access, browser extensions, screen capture, and a cloud-synced Documents folder are outside the app's protection. Check that this BudgetMe folder is not synced or shared. Encrypted files can be copied and attacked offline.

Auto-lock follows 15 minutes without an authenticated action; simply reading the page does not extend it. The app cannot guarantee zeroization of all JavaScript memory or secure erasure on an SSD. Closing the browser alone does not stop the server; lock first or stop the launcher.
